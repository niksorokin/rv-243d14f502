04 · INSIGHTS

Three insights from the week.

Three recurring signals from the "What repeats" section: they explain why the first automations should be small and embedded into existing tools.

01

Numbers flow into messages, tickets, or spreadsheets, and then back into numbers

This cycle is visible in High Tariffs, B2B in Mexico City, Intercity in Bogota, Nairobi, and Guadalajara XL. The most practical offload is drafting work messages and comments from already open data.

02

Telegram is the work layer between analytics and actions

Chats hold decisions, owners, and clarifications that then need to move to spreadsheets, the tracker, or meetings. Therefore, the first automation is careful extraction of tasks and next actions from conversations.

03

Tasks rarely close in one session

~650 minutes belong to tasks visible across both days. The expensive part is not the work itself, but re-entering context after switching between countries, tariffs, meetings, and FleetHub.
